# VIT Chennai BCHY101P Engineering Chemistry Lab Syllabus (2026–27)

## Grading Structure
- Internal: 60M (8 regular exps × 10M + 20M quiz over 10 exps = 100M scaled to 60M)
- FAT: 50M (40M experiment + 10M short procedure, scaled to 40M)
- Error tolerances:
  - Titration: ≤2% (10M), 2–3% (9M), 3–4% (8M), >4% (5M)
  - Instrument: ≤3% (10M), 3–4% (9M), 4–5% (8M), >5% (5M)

## 10 Experiments Matrix
1. Comprehensive Water Analysis
   - Part A: EDTA Hardness (Std Hardwater 1mg/mL CaCO3, EBT, NH3-NH4Cl buffer pH 10, wine-red → steel-blue)
   - Part B: Chloride Conductometric Titration (AgNO3 vs NaCl, AgCl precipitate, L/V conductivity curve)
   - Part C: TDS via Electrical Conductivity (TDS = 0.7 × EC μS/cm)
   - Part D: Sulfate via Nephelometer (BaCl2 + SO4(2-) → BaSO4, NTU calibration 50–250 ppm)
   - Report: BIS drinking water limits (Hardness 200, Cl 250, TDS 500, SO4 200 ppm)
2. Heavy Metal Removal via Eggshell HAP Nanoparticles
   - HAP synthesis: Eggshell + HNO3 + (NH4)2HPO4 (pH 9) → 900°C 3h anneal → Ca10(PO4)6(OH)2
   - XRD: Cu Kα (λ=1.54060 Å), Scherrer crystallite size D = (0.9 λ) / (β cos θ)
   - Part B: Ni(2+) colorimetry via DMG + K3[Fe(CN)6] at λ=440 nm; Batch adsorption % Removal = (C0 - Ce)/C0 × 100
3. Flexible Dye Sensitized Solar Cells (DSSC)
   - Natural dye (pomegranate/beetroot) + TiO2 photoanode on ITO-PET + Carbon cathode + KI/I2 redox (3I(-) / I3(-))
   - Multimeter V_dark vs V_light, 5mm LED circuit
4. Galvanic Cells & Thermodynamic Functions (ΔG, ΔH, ΔS)
   - Zn-Cu Daniell Cell, SCE reference (0.244V), Nernst eq with ion activity coefficients γc
   - ΔG = -nFE_cell, ΔS = nF(∂E/∂T), ΔH = ΔG + TΔS
5. Ethyl Acetate Hydrolysis Kinetics
   - Pseudo-first-order: CH3COOC2H5 + H2O -(HCl)-> CH3COOH + C2H5OH
   - Aliquot titration with 0.2N NaOH (phenolphthalein), k1 = (2.303/t) log[(V∞ - V0)/(V∞ - Vt)]
6. Mg Salt Water Activated Battery
   - Mg anode (oxidation) + Cu cathode (reduction), 5% NaCl electrolyte
   - Single-cell and 6-cell series battery powering 6–9V LED
7. Cu2O Nanoparticles Size-Dependent Color
   - Benedict's reagent + glucose under varying [NaOH] (0 to 0.1M), yellow → red shift
   - Nephelometric turbidity (NTU) vs NaOH volume, back-calculation of Cu(II)
8. Al-Doped ZnO Nanoparticles (Sol-Gel)
   - Zn(NO3)2 + Al(NO3)3 + PVA capping agent (70°C), drying 150°C 12h, annealing 600°C 2h
   - Characterization: XRD wurtzite peak shifts, SEM morphology, UV-Vis bandgap
9. Rhodamine B Degradation Kinetics (UV/H2O2 AOP)
   - UV photolysis of H2O2 -> 2 •OH radicals (2.8 eV oxidant)
   - Pseudo-first-order dye degradation monitored at λ=543 nm, ln(At/A0) = -kt
10. State of Charge (SoC) Determination
    - Li-ion cells (LFP vs NMC, 18650/23650)
    - SoC = (Q_available / Q_rated) × 100%, OCV relaxation and OCV vs SoC discharge curves
